FAQ

Questions buyers often ask.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

For our in-stock designs, MOQ starts from small batches per style. For fully custom OEM/ODM plush, MOQ is typically 500–1,000 pcs per design depending on size and materials. Tell us your quantity and we'll find a workable plan.

Can you make a plush toy from my drawing or logo?

Yes. Send us a sketch, character artwork, photos or even a reference product. Our designers turn it into a production-ready pattern, and we confirm every detail with you on a physical sample before mass production.

How long does sampling and production take?

Samples usually take 7–15 days after artwork confirmation. Mass production typically takes 25–45 days depending on quantity and complexity. Our digital production tracking keeps you updated at each stage.

Which safety standards can you produce to?

We produce to international toy-safety requirements and can arrange third-party testing to EN71 (EU), ASTM F963 / CPSIA (US) and other market standards on request.

Can you add sound, light or other functions?

Yes — we can add voice boxes, LED lights, heat packs, magnets, keychains and more. Custom hang-tags, labels, packaging and barcodes are also available.

What are your payment and shipping terms?

Standard terms are 30% deposit and 70% balance before shipment; other terms can be discussed for long-term partners. We ship FOB / EXW / CIF / DDP by sea, air or express worldwide.
