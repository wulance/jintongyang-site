About us

Two decades of soft-toy craft.

Hebei Jintongyang Toys Co., Ltd. is an integrated plush toy manufacturer in Neiqiu Industrial Park, Xingtai — combining original design, large-scale production and global sales.

Our story

Built on craft, scaled for global buyers.

With more than 20 years of experience in plush manufacturing and brand OEM, we are one of Northern China's dedicated plush toy producers. We own two in-house brands and have long served well-known domestic and international labels.

Our range covers pets, wild animals and ocean creatures, plus cushions, keychains and promotional plush. We add functions like sound, light and heat packs, and serve supermarkets, e-commerce sellers, gift companies, corporate merchandise and wedding-favour markets.

Whether you need a few hundred custom mascots or a container of claw-machine plush every month, we schedule production flexibly to match your business.
